TEXTILE INTELLIGENCE

The Art of Selecting Your Drape

❖

Understanding the feel, weight, and character of each weave helps you choose the perfect outfit for your milestone.

The South Silk Presence

South Silks hold an unmatched structural grandeur. The contrast borders and temple korvai pallu frame the wearer with traditional majesty, making them quintessential for weddings and deep-rooted rituals.

The Banarasi Luminosity

Banarasi sarees catch the evening light with exceptional shimmer through silver and gold zari jaal. They drape with regal fluidity, ideal for sangeet nights, receptions, and anniversary galas.

The Bandhani Warmth

Each tiny tied knot (bindi) reveals the touch of human hands. Bandhani sarees have organic give and stretch, creating a flattering drape that breathes festive cheer into Navratri, Diwali, and family poojas.
