# 1. Instructions to the AI Agent

You are creating a premium, trustworthy, customer-focused website for
**Radha Raman Ethnics**, a boutique ethnic-wear business in Sola,
Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

Treat this document as the primary business specification. Do not mix
this business with another client, brand or website. Do not carry over
Desi Vibe branding, US-oriented copy, lehenga-only positioning,
placeholder business details, fake products, prices, testimonials or
addresses from any starter template.

## 1.1 Primary objective

Build a beautiful, fast, mobile-first boutique website that helps
customers:

1.  Understand what Radha Raman Ethnics offers.
2.  Discover sarees and other ethnic-wear categories.
3.  See the boutique's visual style and latest collection inspiration.
4.  Enquire about a garment, design, size, colour, availability or
    occasion.
5.  Book a compulsory appointment before visiting.
6.  Request a video-call shopping session.
7.  Ask about worldwide shipping and delivery arrangements.
8.  Contact the business through WhatsApp or phone.
9.  Follow the official Instagram and Facebook profiles.

The website is primarily a **lead-generation and assisted-shopping
website**, not an assumed e-commerce checkout platform. Do not add cart,
online payment, live stock, automatic shipping rates or instant purchase
claims unless the business later confirms that those systems exist.

## 1.2 Decision hierarchy

When information conflicts, use this order:

1.  New written instructions from the business owner.
2.  Current business information confirmed directly by the owner.
3.  This master document's explicitly marked business facts.
4.  Official Radha Raman Ethnics social media posts and profile
    information.
5.  Clearly labelled recommendations in this document.
6.  Generic assumptions --- never use these as factual business claims.

If a required detail is unknown, omit it or show a neutral enquiry
prompt. Do not invent it.

## 1.3 Output expectations

The final website should be:

-   Premium, elegant, warm and culturally rooted.
-   Visually led, with excellent saree and outfit photography.
-   Mobile-first, since social-media visitors are likely to arrive on
    phones.
-   Easy for a non-technical boutique operator to maintain.
-   Designed around appointment booking and direct enquiries.
-   Accessible, responsive and performant.
-   SEO-ready for local discovery in Sola and Ahmedabad, without keyword
    stuffing.
-   Honest about stock, prices, shipping, appointment requirements and
    product details.

# 3. Business Model and Customer Experience

## 3.1 Operating model

The business is described as an in-house boutique operation managed by a
woman. The website should therefore make direct personal assistance and
appointment-based service feel like strengths, without inventing staff
size or claiming a large retail operation.

Recommended service model:

1.  Customer discovers the brand on Instagram, Facebook, Google or the
    website.
2.  Customer browses categories and collection imagery.
3.  Customer clicks WhatsApp, calls or submits a short enquiry.
4.  The business responds and confirms product details.
5.  For an in-person visit, the customer books an appointment.
6.  For remote customers, the business arranges a video-call shopping
    interaction where mutually agreed.
7.  Shipping details, charges, timelines and payment arrangements are
    confirmed directly before order commitment.

## 3.2 Key conversion actions

Primary CTA: - **Book Your Appointment**

Secondary CTAs: - **Explore Collections** - **Enquire on WhatsApp** -
**Shop by Video Call** - **Ask About Worldwide Shipping** - **Call the
Boutique** - **View Instagram Collection**

Every page should have at least one useful next action. Avoid competing
CTAs with identical visual priority.

## 3.3 Appointment-only policy

This is a critical operating detail and must be clearly communicated.

Recommended copy: \> **Visits are by appointment only.** Please contact
Radha Raman Ethnics before visiting so the boutique can coordinate a
suitable time.

Place this notice: - In the homepage hero or immediately below it. - In
the header announcement strip or a concise appointment notice. - On the
contact page. - Near the address and map section. - In the appointment
enquiry form. - In the footer's visit information.

Do not show generic "Open now" or walk-in shopping language. Do not
invent opening hours.

## 3.4 Video-call shopping

Recommended copy: \> Shopping from another city or country? Connect with
Radha Raman Ethnics to enquire about a personal video-call shopping
session and explore available styles remotely.

Do not claim instant video-call availability, 24/7 service, a particular
video platform or a guaranteed response time unless confirmed.

## 3.5 Worldwide shipping

Recommended copy: \> Worldwide shipping is available. Contact us to
confirm delivery availability, shipping charges, dispatch timelines,
customs or import duties, and other order details for your destination.

Do not promise: - Free worldwide shipping. - Delivery to every
country. - A fixed delivery time. - Guaranteed customs clearance. - Free
returns or exchanges. - Insurance or tracking unless confirmed.

## 6.4 Typography

Recommended pairing: - Display headings: `Playfair Display` or
`Cormorant Garamond`. - Body/UI: `DM Sans`, `Lato` or `Inter`. - Logo:
use the original logo artwork; never recreate the script wordmark with a
substitute font.

Typography hierarchy: - Desktop hero heading: approximately 56--76 px. -
Mobile hero heading: approximately 38--48 px. - Section headings:
approximately 34--54 px. - Body copy: 15--18 px. - Navigation and
labels: 11--13 px with restrained letter spacing. - Buttons: 11--13 px,
medium/bold.

Use a maximum of two website font families plus the logo artwork.

## 6.5 Logo usage

Primary logo: - Use the horizontal logo on desktop header and footer
where enough space exists. - Use the circular RR monogram for social
avatars, favicon and compact mobile contexts. - Maintain adequate clear
space around the logo. - Do not place it on visually busy photography
without a suitable solid/transparent backing. - Never crop the peacock
feather, tagline or wordmark unintentionally. - Do not use the logo as a
background watermark behind body text.

Recommended logo files to maintain: - `logo-primary.svg` or transparent
PNG. - `logo-monogram.svg` or transparent PNG. - `logo-light.svg` for
dark backgrounds if owner approves. - `favicon.ico` and square 512×512
PNG for web/app icons.

## 6.6 Photography art direction

Photography is one of the most important conversion elements.

Preferred: - Actual business collection photos. - Full-length outfit
photographs. - Saree drape on a model or mannequin, with permission. -
Close-up of pallu, border, weave, embroidery and fabric texture. -
Folded saree stacks arranged neatly. - Natural light and warm editorial
lighting. - Premium boutique interiors or packaging only if they belong
to the business. - Consistent neutral, ivory, warm beige or
heritage-inspired backgrounds.

Stock photography: - May be used for editorial mood sections and general
cultural context. - Must not be presented as actual inventory. - Never
imply a stock image is a product currently available at the boutique. -
Prefer owned product images on collection and product enquiry pages. -
Maintain a record of image source, licence and usage rights.

Image quality: - Product hero: 4:5 or 3:4 portrait. - Collection cards:
4:5 portrait. - Editorial banners: 16:9 or 3:2. - Social avatar: square
1:1. - Use WebP or AVIF with responsive `srcset` where possible. -
Lazy-load below-the-fold images. - Set explicit width and height to
prevent layout shifts. - Write meaningful alt text, not keyword-stuffed
alt text.

# 13. Technical Implementation Standards

## 13.1 Recommended stack

For a lightweight brochure and lead-generation site: - Semantic HTML5. -
Modern CSS3. - Vanilla JavaScript or a lightweight component approach. -
Bootstrap or Tailwind only if the project already uses it
consistently. - No unnecessary heavy framework. - A small backend only
if appointment requests, content management or inventory administration
require it.

If the existing static HTML starter is reused: - Audit every page and
asset. - Remove all previous business branding and demo content. - Use a
shared header, footer, CSS tokens and JavaScript. - Avoid duplicate
page-specific CSS. - Ensure all navigation and CTA links work. - Do not
leave example.com canonical tags or placeholder metadata.

## 13.2 Performance

Targets: - Aim for Core Web Vitals in Google's "good" ranges. - LCP: 2.5
seconds or less. - INP: 200 ms or less. - CLS: 0.1 or less.

Implementation: - Compress images and use WebP/AVIF. - Responsive image
sizes. - Lazy-load below-the-fold images. - Preload only the true hero
image if beneficial. - Avoid autoplay video backgrounds on mobile. -
Minify CSS/JS for production. - Limit third-party scripts. - Use
font-display swap. - Avoid layout shifts from images and fonts. - Cache
static assets appropriately.

## 13.3 Accessibility

-   Keyboard-operable navigation and forms.
-   Visible focus states.
-   Sufficient text contrast.
-   Labels for all form fields.
-   Helpful validation messages.
-   Alt text for meaningful images.
-   Empty alt for decorative images.
-   Reduced-motion support.
-   Buttons must have accessible names.
-   Avoid text embedded only in images.
-   Ensure touch targets are comfortable on mobile.

## 13.4 Security and privacy

-   HTTPS only.
-   Validate and sanitize any server-side form input.
-   Spam protection for public forms.
-   Do not expose API keys.
-   Do not collect unnecessary personal information.
-   Provide a privacy policy if personal data is collected.
-   Explain what happens when a user clicks WhatsApp.
-   Avoid third-party tracking without appropriate notice/consent where
    required.
-   Do not store customer phone numbers in client-side JavaScript or
    public files.
