1. Boutique Appointment Policy

Radha Raman Ethnics is an in-house boutique hosted at C-402, Arise Pride, Sola, Ahmedabad. All in-person boutique visits are strictly by appointment only. An appointment request submitted via this website does not constitute a confirmed visit until approved by Radha Raman Ethnics over WhatsApp or telephone.

2. Assisted Shopping & Product Enquiries

Our website is designed for collection discovery and assisted personal consultation. Because our sarees and ethnic wear are curated in selective artisan batches, real-time availability, fabric nuances, and pricing are confirmed directly with founder Pinaki Patel prior to transaction commitment.

3. Artisanal Textile Characteristics

Handcrafted Indian textiles, including Banarasi brocades, South Silk weaves, and Bandhani resist tie-dyes, inherently feature subtle characteristics of manual human creation. Minor variations in weave density, zari shading, and knot alignment reflect authentic artisan craftsmanship and are cherished traits of handlooms.

4. Remote Orders & Worldwide Delivery

Domestic and international shipping options are arranged following personal consultation. Shipping transit timelines are estimates provided by international courier services (such as DHL, FedEx, or India Post) and may vary due to destination customs procedures or regional holidays. Applicable destination customs duties or import taxes are determined by recipient country laws and are payable by the recipient.

5. Jurisdiction

Any disputes or inquiries relating to our services or boutique consultations shall be governed under the laws of Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.
