CUSTOMER TRUST & DATA INTEGRITY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Radha Raman Ethnics, Sola, Ahmedabad

1. Overview

At Radha Raman Ethnics, we deeply value the trust you place in our boutique. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is gathered, utilized, and safeguarded when interacting with our website, booking appointments, or conversing with our team.

2. Direct Communication Model

Our website functions as an assisted-shopping discovery platform. When you submit an appointment request, video-shopping booking, or saree inquiry, the information you provide (name, phone number, occasion, and collection interests) is utilized strictly to draft a direct WhatsApp message or initiate phone communication with founder Pinaki Patel. We do not maintain unencrypted remote marketing databases or sell your data to any third party.

3. Cookies & Analytics

This website does not deploy invasive cross-site tracking cookies or third-party behavioral advertising scripts. Basic server logs or privacy-conscious analytics may record device type, general location, and page visits solely to ensure website performance and responsiveness.

4. International Orders & Shipping Data

For customers requesting domestic or worldwide courier dispatch, recipient name, delivery address, contact number, and postal code are shared exclusively with accredited courier partners (such as DHL Express, FedEx, or India Post) strictly for customs clearance and package delivery.
